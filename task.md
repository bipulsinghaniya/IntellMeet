IntellMeet AI-Powered Enterprise Meeting & Collaboration Platform
IntellMeet is an AI-powered enterprise meeting and collaboration platform developed using the MERN stack to enhance communication and productivity for modern remote and hybrid teams. The platform provides real-time video conferencing, live chat, screen sharing, AI-generated meeting summaries, automatic transcription, smart action item extraction, and collaborative team workspaces within a secure and scalable environment. Built with technologies like React, Node.js, MongoDB, Socket.io, WebRTC, and OpenAI integration, IntellMeet delivers a production-grade experience with enterprise-level security, real-time performance, cloud scalability, and intelligent workflow automation, helping organizations transform meetings into actionable and efficient business outcomes.
