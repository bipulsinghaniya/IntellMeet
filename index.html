Enterprise Architecture

Built for Real-Time Speed & AI Automation

IntellMeet is engineered on modern web foundations to deliver sub-second collaborative workflows without compromising on security or scale.

WebRTC SFU Video Grid

Selective Forwarding Unit (SFU) architecture capable of handling multi-party video conferencing with dynamic bandwidth adaptation and packet loss concealment.

Speech Diarization

Multi-speaker voice fingerprinting distinguishes between participants in real time, assigning accurate speaker labels and timestamps instantly.

Continuous AI Summarization

Generative transformers synthesize live discussions into structured decision matrices, key takeaways, and action items as the meeting progresses.

Smart Action Item Extraction

NLP pipelines automatically identify commitments, assignees, deadlines, and urgency levels, syncing directly with Jira, Linear, and Trello.

Enterprise-Grade Security

End-to-End Encryption (E2EE), SOC-2 Type II readiness, role-based access control (RBAC), and customer-managed KMS encryption keys.

MERN Scalability

Built with React, Node.js, Socket.io, and MongoDB Atlas with automatic sharding and real-time bidirectional socket channels.
